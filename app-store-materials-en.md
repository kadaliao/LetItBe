## Description

**For those low-energy moments when you need support without pressure.**

LetItBe offers short, carefully crafted support cards for when you're exhausted, overwhelmed, or just running on empty. No motivational speeches, no guilt trips—just honest, gentle words that meet you where you are.

**Core Features:**
- Four mood states with curated content
- One-tap card refresh within the same state
- Optional "repair" actions (breathing, simple checklists)
- Completely offline and privacy-first

**Perfect for:**
- Late nights when energy is low
- Moments of self-doubt or inner noise
- When you know you should rest but can't
- Times when typical "positive thinking" feels empty

**What LetItBe is NOT:**
- Not a therapy replacement
- Not a productivity tool
- Not about goals or self-improvement
- No tracking, no social features, no pressure

**Privacy:**
- All data stays on your device
- No account required
- No usage tracking or analytics
- No data collection

This is an app that respects your limits. It won't push you to be better—it just offers a buffer when things feel too hard.

## FAQ for App Store / Website

**Q: Is this a medical or therapy app?**
A: No. LetItBe is an emotional support tool, not a replacement for therapy, medical advice, or mental health treatment. If you're experiencing a crisis, please contact a mental health professional or crisis hotline.

**Q: Does the app collect my data?**
A: No. All data stays on your device. We don't collect usage data, don't require an account, and don't track your behavior.

**Q: Do I need an internet connection?**
A: No. The app works completely offline.

**Q: Can I add my own cards or customize content?**
A: Not in v1.0. The current version focuses on a curated set of cards. We may consider this in future versions based on user feedback.

**Q: Why only four mood states?**
A: We intentionally kept it minimal to reduce cognitive load. The goal is to get you to supportive content quickly, not to make you analyze your emotional state in detail.

**Q: Is there a subscription or cost?**
A: The app is currently free with all features unlocked. No in-app purchases or subscriptions.

**Q: What are "repair" actions?**
A: Simple, low-effort actions designed to prevent things from getting worse—like a brief breathing exercise or a tiny checklist (drink water, wash face). They're optional and take 1-2 minutes.

**Q: Will there be Android support?**
A: Not currently planned, but we're open to it based on demand.

**Q: How do I provide feedback?**
A: Visit our GitHub repository or email [your-email].
